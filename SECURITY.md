# Security & Privacy Guidelines

### Data Storage & Student Privacy
- Should I Bunk runs offline-first.
- All course details and attendance marks stay on your personal device.
- No personal data or attendance statistics are shared with outside servers.
- Notifications are managed locally on your phone.

### Vulnerability Reports
If you notice any bug or security flaw, please contact the maintainer directly at nikunjnehu@gmail.com instead of opening a public issue. We will review and address your report promptly.
